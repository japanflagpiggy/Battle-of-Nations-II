How to Play

Choosing a Nation

Select any nation on Earth to lead. Every other nation is governed by an independent AI, each pursuing its own economic growth, diplomatic standing, and military ambitions.

The Map

Click any territory to open its file: population, treasury, stability, military strength, and its standing with you. Nameplates float above each territory as you zoom in.

Diplomacy

From a nation's file you can Declare War, Request Peace, Send Economic Funds, Improve Relations, or propose an Alliance once relations reach +50. Actions grey out when they don't apply — you can't ally a nation you're at war with, and Declare War locks into a truce for a time after peace is made.

War

Declaring war draws an animated arrow from your territory toward the target. Wars run their course over time; the side with the stronger military, industry, and stability ultimately prevails. The defeated nation is absorbed as a colony of the victor — sharing its color and funding its new master — while any colonies the loser once held are set free.

Governing

Use the Economy panel to set tax policy, the Decisions panel for one-off national actions, and the Political Focus Tree to steer your nation's long-term direction along economic, military, or diplomatic lines.

The AI

AI nations do not wage war at random. They only move against a land neighbor or a nation reachable across the sea — and when a conquest changes the map, that frontier shifts with it.
